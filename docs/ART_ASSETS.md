# Asset hình ảnh 0.2

Tạo bằng công cụ imagegen tích hợp; không dùng CLI/API key, không tải ảnh từ website ngoài. Hai ảnh được tối ưu WebP và đóng gói trong web/Android. Sprite cây, gà, nhân vật nhỏ, icon, hiệu ứng và các trạng thái bản đồ là SVG/CSS trong source.

- `public/art/valley-world.webp`: cảnh thế giới (720×1080).
- `public/art/linh-portrait.webp`: chân dung Linh (600×900), dùng trong khung chân dung/đối thoại.
- `src/components/FarmMap.jsx`: lớp gameplay động, các vùng chạm bám tọa độ cảnh.
- `src/components/Produce.jsx`: nông sản vẽ bằng SVG.

## Prompt cảnh thế giới

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: actual playable portrait mobile farming game environment background, NO interface, NO typography. Create a beautiful original premium cozy Vietnamese countryside farm seen from a high isometric 3/4 camera. Portrait aspect 2:3, full bleed, richly detailed soft painted 3D storybook art, tactile timber and terracotta, lush jade and olive foliage, golden sun, cream highlights, softly shaded forms, charming crafted materials. Clear legible composition designed for interactive overlays: winding sandy footpaths connecting five separate sites with generous space between them. A small red terracotta roof farmhouse with porch sits upper center at x50% y22%; a round blue water reservoir with wooden supports and simple pipe at x20% y44%; a warm wood chicken coop with straw yard at x79% y45%; three EMPTY dark brown rectangular garden beds with neat ridges at lower left x32% y68%, the empty beds must occupy a generous unobstructed area for game-rendered crops; an attractive tiny village market stall with green-and-cream striped cloth awning and empty baskets at lower right x78% y73%. A little stone bridge crosses a turquoise creek at lower center edge. Surround with wildflowers, fruit trees, split rail fences, a field bicycle, baskets, watering can and tiny environmental storytelling. Top fifth shows rolling tea-green hills, hazy mountains and a little sky, bottom grassy foreground. Far background softly atmospheric, playable zone sharply readable. Do not draw crops in garden beds. Do not draw people or chickens: these will be animated separately in engine. No floating island, no cutaway diorama base, no icons, no pins, no labels, no UI cards, no words or watermark. Inviting world with real depth and intricate original art direction, suitable for a paid cozy indie farming game. Buildings unobscured by trees. The scene should look alive and warm, not low-poly flat vector.

## Prompt nhân vật Linh

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original cozy farming videogame character portrait for dialogue UI, transparent PNG cutout. A charming friendly young adult Vietnamese woman named Linh, expressive kind warm brown eyes, naturally proportioned appealing stylized face, subtle freckles and rosy cheeks, short wavy black hair tied into a low side braid, little terracotta hair ribbon, a woven straw sun hat, cream rolled-sleeve blouse under moss-green farmer overalls, holding a little wicker basket containing tomatoes and greens. Portrait from waist upwards facing slightly left toward the viewer with a welcoming, confident smile, one hand resting on the basket. Premium hand-painted 3D storybook game aesthetic, soft brushed detail, natural shapes, tactile fabric and wicker textures, warm golden sunlight from upper left, sophisticated green cream terracotta palette. Original character with personality, not a logo or an icon. Centered portrait occupying nearly all canvas, clear face and silhouette, no background, genuine transparent alpha around character, no panel, no typography, no watermark.

Kết quả chân dung trả về có nền, nên được dùng trong khung/crop có chủ đích, không được mô tả như sprite alpha. Không có nhân vật thương hiệu, ảnh người thật hoặc asset lấy từ game khác.
